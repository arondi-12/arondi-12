## Plans (from /pricing)
- Starter: NGN 0/month, no card. 1 workspace, 3 cohorts, up to 5 teachers/admins, limited students per cohort, limited AI.
- Pro: NGN 20K/month. Unlimited cohorts, up to 500 students per cohort, live classes, AI study assistants, admin moderation.
- Enterprise: custom, "let's talk". Security and compliance tools, institution-level control, custom integrations, dedicated onboarding, priority support.
- Mismatch: the plan cards say Starter / Pro / Enterprise; the comparison table header says Starter / Growth / Enterprise.
- "Everything in Zedu Free" and "Everything in Zedu Educator" name plans that do not exist.

## Product facts (from /products/*)
- Buzz: one-click voice conversation inside any channel; no scheduling; AI summary after each session; multiple participants.
- Channels: organised by course, cohort or topic; threaded replies; announcements.
- File management: store, share, search across courses and files; per-file sharing inside channels; described as "share files securely, control all access".
- /download names: Buzz (Live Huddles), Channels, File Management, Assignments & Updates; a desktop download is offered as well as mobile.
