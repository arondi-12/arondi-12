## 2. About section (max 2,000 characters)

> Zedu is built for bootcamps and learning communities: one workspace where a cohort's channels, live conversations, files and assignments live together instead of across four different apps.
>
> Every cohort gets its own structured channels, organised by course, cohort or topic, with threaded replies so a question does not disappear under the next announcement. When a discussion needs voices rather than typing, Buzz starts a quick voice conversation inside the channel with one click, with no scheduling, and a summary is generated automatically when the session ends.
>
> Course materials sit in the same place as the conversation: files are stored by course, department or cohort, shared directly inside channels, searchable across courses and topics, and access is controlled by the workspace.
>
> AI in Zedu is there to help the people teaching, not replace them: assistants summarise discussions, answer student questions and highlight key information in documents.
>
> Pricing is per programme, not per learner. There are no per-student fees, the Starter plan is free with up to 3 cohorts, and Pro gives unlimited cohorts.
>
> Conversations, recordings and resources are stored securely and available only to authorised members of the workspace.

About length: 1,239 characters (limit 2,000).

## 4. Six launch posts

Each post is written for a bootcamp operator, not for a general audience, and each link returns HTTP 200 today.

**Post 1 — Starter to Pro**
> Most bootcamps do not outgrow their tools in one jump. Starter gives you up to 3 cohorts, organised channels and file sharing for ₦0. When the fourth cohort arrives, Pro is ₦20K a month with unlimited cohorts, live classes, AI study assistants and admin moderation tools.
> Start with 3 cohorts and see how far it goes: https://zedu.chat/pricing

**Post 2 — Flat Naira pricing**
> Per-student pricing punishes a bootcamp for growing. Zedu prices the programme, not the learner: no per-student fees, Starter free with up to 3 cohorts, Pro at ₦20K a month for unlimited cohorts.
> The plans in full: https://zedu.chat/pricing

**Post 3 — Cohort channels**
> A cohort's questions should not compete with announcements in one long thread. Channels keep discussions organised by course, cohort or topic, with threaded replies and announcements posted where they belong.
> How channels work: https://zedu.chat/products/channels

**Post 4 — Buzz**
> Some questions take 30 seconds to answer out loud and ten minutes to type. Buzz starts a voice conversation inside any course or cohort channel with one click, with no scheduling and no separate meeting link.
> Start a Buzz: https://zedu.chat/products/buzz

**Post 5 — AI summaries**
> When a live session ends, the notes usually do not get written. After every Buzz session Zedu generates a summary of the conversation, so the key points stay with the cohort instead of in someone's memory.
> See how it works: https://zedu.chat/products/buzz

**Post 6 — Fewer tools**
> Channels for discussion, Buzz for voice, file management for materials, assignments for submissions: four jobs that usually mean four apps and four logins for a cohort.
> One workspace instead: https://zedu.chat/products/file-management
