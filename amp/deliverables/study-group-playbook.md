# Study-group leader activation playbook: Jan–Feb 2027 exam season

**For:** student union leaders and faculty advisors
**Plan:** free (Starter, ₦0/month) — every feature below was checked against zedu.chat/pricing and the product pages on 22 October 2026.
**Status:** proposed, unpublished. Sources are noted inline; the confirmation list at the end holds everything I could not verify.

This playbook starts after setup. The seven-step setup itself is in the five-minute guide, and the campaign posts and video are separate. Nothing here needs a paid plan.

---

## 1. What the free plan actually gives you

Read this before you promise your group anything, because two things students expect are not included.

| Item | Free (Starter) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Workspaces | 1 | /pricing comparison table |
| Cohorts | 3 | /pricing |
| Teachers / admins | Up to 5 | /pricing |
| Students per cohort | "Limited" (the exact cap is not published) | /pricing |
| Channels, threads, direct messages, group discussions, announcement channels | Included | /pricing |
| File sharing, assignment discussions, course channels | Included | /pricing |
| AI study assistant, AI learning assistants | Limited | /pricing |
| Workspace permissions, basic admin controls | Included | /pricing |
| **Voice and video calls, screen sharing, class recordings** | **Not included** | /pricing (blank in the Starter column, ticked from Pro) |
| Shared resources library, AI message summaries, analytics dashboard | Not included | /pricing |

**What this means in practice:** plan the group's work as text plus files. There is no free voice room and no recording, so a "revision call" has to happen somewhere else or be replaced by a written thread. Do not tell students to start a voice session on the free plan.

**Three cohorts is the ceiling.** One cohort per study group is the simplest structure. If a faculty advisor needs more than three groups in one workspace, each group should create its own free workspace and the advisor joins as a member — but check the student cap in the app first (see the confirmation list).

## 2. Week-by-week timeline

Weeks count backwards from the first exam paper, because a published study week is the exception: only Ilorin has one for 2027 (25–29 January). The anchor dates come from the approved calendar work already on this campaign: Ilorin's Harmattan exams run **1–26 February 2027**; LASU, ABUAD and Nile also examine in the January–February window on their latest published calendars; UNILAG, UNIBEN, ABU Zaria, UI and UNN examine later and belong to a March–June wave.

| Week | Leader does | Group does | Signal it worked |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T-4** (e.g. week of 5 Jan) | Recruit two co-leaders, create the workspace, name it `SS27-<course>`, write three ground rules, fix a check-in day | Nothing yet — they are not invited | Workspace exists, name follows the pattern |
| **T-3** (week of 12 Jan) | Invite the group, post the "start here" message, open one channel per paper | Join, introduce themselves in one line, name the paper that worries them most | Every member has posted once |
| **T-2** (week of 19 Jan) | Assign one paper or topic per member, post the first timed past question | Work the past question, post the answer or the sticking point | At least half the group replies to the check-in |
| **T-1** (lecture-free week if you have one; Ilorin: 25–29 Jan) | Run a daily check-in, one question a day | Answer one question a day, flag the topic they still cannot do | Check-in replies stay flat or rise through the week |
| **Exam weeks** (Ilorin: 1–26 Feb) | Two check-ins a week, no new material | Post one question they are stuck on within an hour of the paper | Questions answered the same day |
| **After the last paper** | Send the survey, write a five-line handover for next semester | Answer six questions, anonymously | Survey replies ÷ group size |

If your faculty's exam dates are not published yet, count backwards from the date the department announces and say "date to be confirmed" in the group rather than repeating last year's date.

## 3. Weekly check-in prompts (copy and paste)

Each prompt is under 45 words and ends with a reply format, so counting replies is mechanical and nobody has to be named.

**Week T-3 — joining**
> Welcome. Reply in one line: your first name, the paper you are most worried about, and the day you can study. Then open the channel for that paper and say hello there. Nothing else is needed today.

**Week T-2 — first past question**
> This week's paper is in the file for your course. Attempt question 2 on your own first, then post where you got stuck. Reply with ✅ done, ⏳ started, or ❌ not yet. No explanation needed.

**Week T-1 — daily question**
> Question of the day: [topic]. Answer in the thread in two sentences, right or wrong. Reply ✅ if you answered, ❌ if you are skipping today. One question a day, nothing more.

**Exam week — the stuck thread**
> If a paper caught you out, post the exact question within an hour while it is fresh. Someone in the group answers before the next paper. Reply ✅ when you have posted or answered something.

**After the last paper**
> Six anonymous questions in the survey link above. Two minutes. No names, no matric numbers. Reply ✅ when you have sent it.

**Rule for leaders:** never ask for names, phone numbers or matric numbers in a check-in. The reply format exists so a tally can be counted without identifying anyone.

## 4. Resource-sharing guidelines

**Share:** your own notes and summaries; slides the lecturer has released to the class; past questions the department has released; a link to the reading list rather than a copy of a book.

**Do not share:** scanned textbooks, paid course packs, a lecturer's slides that were not released to students, or a classmate's notes without asking them first.

**Name every file the same way:** `COURSE_TOPIC_SOURCE_YEAR.pdf`, for example `MTH201_Integration_PastQ_2024.pdf`. A group that names files consistently can find them in the last week; a group that does not, cannot.

**Credit in one line, every time.** Put this in the message that carries the file:
> Source: [who made it], [course or department], [date]. Shared with permission.

**Before you upload, ask one question:** who can see this file once it is in the channel? Zedu says file sharing lets you control access (/products/file-management), but the exact per-file setting on a free workspace is on the confirmation list below. Until you have checked it in the app, upload only what the whole group is allowed to see.

**Sharing a file is not permission to share it.** Holding a copy is not the same as being allowed to redistribute it, so when in doubt post the link and let each person get their own copy.

## 5. Post-exam feedback survey (six questions, anonymous)

Send it once, after the last paper, and say plainly that it is anonymous.

1. How many of the group's check-ins did you take part in? (none / one or two / most / all)
2. Which single thing helped you most? (open text)
3. What got in the way? (open text)
4. Did the file naming and the past-question threads make revision easier? (yes / no / not sure)
5. Would you join the same group next semester? (yes / no / maybe)
6. What is the one change you would make? (open text)

**Do not add** name, email, phone, matric number or course code fields. If a leader needs to follow up with one person, that person can identify themselves in the open text; the form should not require it.

## 6. How to measure this without collecting student data

Two numbers, both countable from the workspace itself.

**Groups started.** Count workspaces whose name follows `SS27-<course>`, or count cohorts created inside one leader's workspace (maximum three on the free plan). Report the weekly count and the running total.

**Check-in completion.** Each week has one check-in thread. Count the replies carrying ✅, ⏳ or ❌ and divide by the number of people who joined the group. That gives a completion rate per group per week, with no names attached.

Record it in one sheet with four columns and nothing else:

| Group code | Week | Replies counted | Members |
|---|---|---|---|
| SS27-MTH201 | T-2 | 14 | 22 |

**What these numbers cannot tell you**, and should not be presented as if they could: whether anyone attended a physical session, whether marks improved, or who stopped taking part. If the team wants outcomes, that needs a separate consented study, not a check-in thread.

## 7. Leader checklist (one page, print it)

- [ ] Workspace named `SS27-<course>`, three ground rules posted
- [ ] One channel per paper, plus one announcements channel
- [ ] Every member has posted once
- [ ] Files named `COURSE_TOPIC_SOURCE_YEAR`, each with a credit line
- [ ] Check-in day fixed and written in the announcement channel
- [ ] Nothing promised that the free plan does not include: no calls, no recordings
- [ ] Survey sent after the last paper, with no personal-data fields
- [ ] Weekly tally updated
