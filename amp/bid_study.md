A playbook a student union leader can run in a week, built only on features the free plan actually has.

What I will deliver, as one document:
1. A week-by-week timeline for the Jan-Feb 2027 exam season: when to recruit, when to run the first session, the week the group should switch from forming to revising, and the week to stop and ask for feedback. Written by week number and relative to the exam start, not by invented term dates.
2. Copy-ready check-in prompts, one per week, that a leader can paste into a channel: what to bring, what the group will cover, one question to answer before the session, and a short closing line that records whether people came.
3. Resource-sharing guidance: what to put in a channel, what to keep in files, how to name a file so the next cohort finds it, and what not to upload (other people's slides without permission, past papers the university has not released).
4. A six-question post-exam survey and a measurement note that counts groups started and check-in completion from channel activity only. No student names, emails or phone numbers are collected anywhere.

What I checked first (22 Oct 2026): the free Starter plan gives 1 workspace, 3 cohorts, up to 5 teachers/admins and limited students per cohort, with organised channels, threaded replies and file sharing. I will not assume storage caps, recording limits or DM behaviour on the free plan; those go in a marked "confirm before publishing" list rather than into the advice.

What I will not do: repeat the campaign calendar, the five-minute setup guide, the social posts or the video script.

Time: two days. Reviewer check: four components present, every product claim traceable to /pricing or a /products page, and no invented term dates or numbers.
